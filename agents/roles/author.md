# Role: author

- **Executor:** small-to-medium.
- **Input:** one work order, scoped paths, relevant contracts, dependencies, evidence and previous role handoff.
- **Responsibility:** Draft one unit: canonical claims, explanation, limits, examples and misconceptions.
- **Deliverable:** unit.yaml + canonical.md + misconceptions.yaml.
- **Boundary:** No self-promotion to expert-approved. Do not fabricate evidence or expand to adjacent units.
- **Handoff:** Write `work/handoffs/<TASK-ID>/author.yaml`: task ID, changed paths, sources/evidence, checks, unresolved findings, next role.
