# Role: assessment_designer

- **Executor:** small-to-medium + deterministic code.
- **Input:** one work order, scoped paths, relevant contracts, dependencies, evidence and previous role handoff.
- **Responsibility:** Map assessable outcomes to questions; ensure precise keys, rationales, distractors and computational checks.
- **Deliverable:** questions.yaml with linked outcomes and review notes.
- **Boundary:** No secret final exam bank in public git. Do not treat generated answers as proof.
- **Handoff:** Write `work/handoffs/<TASK-ID>/assessment_designer.yaml`: task ID, changed paths, sources/evidence, checks, unresolved findings, next role.
