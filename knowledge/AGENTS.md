# Knowledge author guidance

Read `contracts/CONVENTIONS.md`; edit only your assigned unit directory. Keep claims stable and narrowly scoped; define terms, applicability, examples and limits. Include sources and misconceptions when relevant. Prefer atomic outcomes; one unit is not a full course. If the fact is disputed or version-dependent, flag it. Do not change assessment files unless the order explicitly includes them. No LLM may mark its own content expert-approved.
